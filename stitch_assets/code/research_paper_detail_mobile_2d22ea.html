Abstract

This paper provides an in-depth analysis of the current state of deep learning integration within robotic systems. We examine how neural networks are revolutionizing perception, control, and decision-making processes. The study highlights significant breakthroughs in reinforcement learning for locomotion and the challenges of deploying high-dimensional models in real-time edge environments.
